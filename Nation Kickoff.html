Nation
Kickoff

Pick a national team, line up your formation and play a full 11 v 11 match built for your thumbs. Pass, through-ball, shoot, tackle, celebrate.

Controls
MOVE
Left stick moves your player. Hold Sprint to run faster.
PASS
Hold to charge power: tap for a normal pass, hold longer for a harder one. Defending: switch player.
THRU
Hold to charge a through ball into space for a runner. Defending: slide tackle.
SHOOT
Hold to charge power, aim with the stick, release. Defending: press and tackle.
SKILL
Tap Sprint with the ball for a skill dash past defenders.
Keyboard: WASD or arrows, J pass, K shoot, L through, Shift sprint, Space skill, E switch.
