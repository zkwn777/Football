How to play

MOVE
Left stick moves your player. Hold Sprint to run faster.
PASS
Hold to charge power: tap for a normal pass, hold longer for a harder one. An arrow shows where it goes. Defending: switch player.
THRU
Hold to charge a lofted through ball into space for a runner. Defending: slide tackle.
SHOOT
Hold to charge power, aim with the stick, release. Defending: press and tackle.
SWIPE
Swipe a button up or down while you press it. Pass: up = lofted, down = driven. Thru: up = lob, down = driven. Shoot: up = power shot (hold to charge, risky), down = finesse curler.
HOLD
Defending: hold Pass to contain (shadow the attacker at a safe distance), tap Pass to switch player. With the ball: hold Sprint for a knock-on.
HEAD
When a high ball (a cross or corner) reaches your player, SHOOT and PASS glow blue. Press Shoot to jump and head it at goal, or press Pass (aim at a teammate) to head it to them. At your corner, tap Pass to cross.
SKILL
Tap Sprint with the ball to play your equipped skill move.
VIEW
First person (Settings or Pause): the stick turns you left/right and runs you forward. Passes go to the teammate you look at; shots go where you look.
CURL
Swipe down on Shoot for a finesse curler: it bends around the keeper into the far corner.
Keyboard: WASD or arrows, J pass, K shoot, L through, Shift sprint, Space skill, E switch.
